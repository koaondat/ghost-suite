Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Phantom — licensing, activation, downloads, and account management.

Can't find your answer? Ask on Discord
Phantom is a Windows desktop application used to manage your Phantom license and premium features. It provides a fast, offline-capable interface for configuring system identities, managing network adapters, and activating your license.
  1. Open Phantom.
  2. Go to the License page.
  3. Paste your license key.
  4. Click Activate.
Your license key is available in the following places:
  • Immediately after purchase via the order confirmation page
  • Inside your Dashboard → Purchases
If your key hasn't appeared:
  1. Wait a few moments and then refresh the Purchases page.
  2. If it still doesn't appear, join our Discord and provide your Order ID — a staff member will assist you.
Licenses are normally tied to one PC. If you changed hardware or purchased a new computer, please contact support via Discord and we'll help you transfer your activation.
