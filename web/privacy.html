Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

Phantom collects only the information necessary to process your purchase and deliver your license.

1. Information Collected

We collect your email address and Discord username at checkout. Payment processing is handled by PayPal — we never store card details on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your email is used to identify your order and deliver license keys. Your Discord username is used to grant access to our support server.

3. Data Retention

Order records are retained for accounting purposes. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us on Discord.

4. Third Parties

Payment processing is handled by PayPal. We do not sell or share your data with other third parties.

For questions, contact us via Discord.
