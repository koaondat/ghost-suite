Billing & Licenses

Common billing questions

Everything you need to know before you buy.

  • After purchase, your license key is sent directly to you via Discord DM from the Ghost bot, and also displayed on the confirmation page. Keys are in the format GHOST-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX.

  • Each license key is valid for a single machine at a time. If you need to transfer your license to a new machine, contact support via Discord and we will reissue your key.

  • Your existing key remains active and nothing changes. Renewal is handled automatically. You will receive a receipt and a renewal confirmation in Discord. You can cancel any time from the dashboard.

  • A Lifetime key is issued with a far-future expiry encoded in the HMAC payload and will never require renewal. It includes all future major and minor version updates to Ghost at no additional cost.

  • We offer a 7-day refund window for Pro and Lifetime purchases if Ghost does not work as described on your system. Open a ticket in Discord with your order ID and we will process it promptly.
